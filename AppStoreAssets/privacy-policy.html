Privacy Policy for VolleyballScore

Last updated: October 7, 2026

VolleyballScore ("the App") is a volleyball scorekeeping app. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

Summary

VolleyballScore does not have its own servers, does not run analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not sell or share your data with third parties. The only data the App stores is the match history and live-score information described below, and it is stored using your own iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit framework.

Match history (private to you)

When you finish or end a match, the App saves the result (team names, scores, sets, and timestamps) on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. If you are signed in to iCloud, this history is automatically synced across your own devices using Apple's CloudKit private database. This data is encrypted and accessible only to you, the same way Apple's Notes or Reminders apps sync your content. The developer of this App cannot see, access, or receive a copy of your match history.

Live broadcast ("Watch" feature)

VolleyballScore lets a scorekeeper share live scores with spectators using a temporary 6-character join code. While broadcasting, the following information is written to Apple's CloudKit public database, associated only with that join code (not with your Apple ID or identity):

  • Team names and the live score you enter
  • The join code itself
  • A timestamp of the last update

Anyone who is given the join code can view this live score information for as long as the match is being broadcast. The broadcaster can stop broadcasting at any time, which deletes this public record. Join codes are short-lived and are not linked to any personal account information.

Spectators who tap "Watch" send a small periodic "heartbeat" signal (a random identifier generated on-device, stored only in that device's local app settings) so the broadcaster can see an approximate count of how many devices are currently watching. This identifier is not linked to your name, Apple ID, or any other personal information, and is not used for tracking across apps or websites.

WhatsApp sharing (optional)

If you choose to share a score update via WhatsApp, the App opens WhatsApp (or wa.me in your browser) with a pre-filled text message. You must manually tap "Send" inside WhatsApp — the App does not access your contacts and cannot send messages on your behalf. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to anything you send through WhatsApp.

What we don't do

  • We don't use advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't require you to create an account or provide your name, email, or phone number to use the App.
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties.

Children's privacy

VolleyballScore does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not require sign-up and does not collect any personal information beyond what is described above.

Your choices

  • You can delete any saved match from the History tab at any time.
  • You can stop broadcasting at any time, which removes the public live-score record.
  • You can control iCloud sync for this App in your device's Settings app (Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the App after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
